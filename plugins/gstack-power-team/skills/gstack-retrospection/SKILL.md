# Retrospection

Modification notice: Rewritten and consolidated by Pablo Poo. Attribution and applicable terms: [NOTICE](../../NOTICE.md).

## Inputs

- work period
- verified outcomes
- shortcuts and follow-up evidence

Use available evidence; identify missing information only when it changes the decision.

## Method

- State reporting period and snapshot date.
- Use verified outcomes rather than intentions.
- Separate measured results, incomplete evidence, and follow-up.
- Record owner, impact, and upgrade trigger for shortcuts. Revisit prior recommendations against current-period evidence and mark them addressed, partial, open, or unassessed; preserve unresolved follow-ups for the next cycle.
- Preserve lessons that change future practice.

## Result

A factual retrospective with owned follow-ups and lessons.

## Scope

Does not treat missing evidence as success.

Apply within the authorized local development scope. Choose means from the project's existing conventions. Scale evidence to the consequence of error and disclose material limitations.
