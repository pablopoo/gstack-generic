# Product Discovery

Modification notice: Rewritten and consolidated by Pablo Poo. Attribution and applicable terms: [NOTICE](../../NOTICE.md).

## Inputs

- idea or opportunity
- target audience
- known evidence and constraints

Use available evidence; identify missing information only when it changes the decision.

## Method

- Name the user, workaround, and observable problem cost.
- Challenge urgency, reachability, differentiation, and the smallest wedge.
- Separate evidence from aspiration; prefer interpretations supported by observed user needs over the most desirable answer.
- Identify the assumption most likely to invalidate the idea.
- Recommend proceed, reshape, defer, or stop.
- Carry valid unresolved concerns across review rounds until evidence resolves or supersedes them. A failed later review or acknowledgement does not erase or fix an earlier concern.

## Result

Problem framing, key assumptions, and a scoped next decision.

## Scope

Discovery judgment only; not a delivery plan.

Apply within the authorized local development scope. Choose means from the project's existing conventions. Scale evidence to the consequence of error and disclose material limitations.
