# Product Challenge

Modification notice: Rewritten and consolidated by Pablo Poo. Attribution and applicable terms: [NOTICE](../../NOTICE.md).

## Inputs

- proposed direction
- evidence of user need
- constraints and alternative uses of effort

Use available evidence; identify missing information only when it changes the decision.

## Method

- Identify the user outcome and assumption that makes the proposal valuable.
- Compare the proposed scope with a smaller alternative and doing nothing. For process artifacts, name their actual reader, changed decision, smallest useful form, and revisit or retirement condition. Explicit user needs and credible preventive risks count; do not require a past incident or artificial implementation consumer.
- Distinguish observed demand from stakeholder preference and aspiration.
- Choose what to retain, defer, reshape, or reject, with evidence that would reverse the choice.

## Result

A scope recommendation with tradeoffs, assumptions, and a decisive next check.

## Scope

Decision review only; preserve stated requirements unless a change is authorized.

Apply within the authorized local development scope. Choose means from the project's existing conventions. Scale evidence to the consequence of error and disclose material limitations.
