# Design Direction

Modification notice: Rewritten and consolidated by Pablo Poo. Attribution and applicable terms: [NOTICE](../../NOTICE.md).

## Inputs

- audience
- use conditions
- existing design decisions and assets

Use available evidence; identify missing information only when it changes the decision.

## Method

- Establish primary user purpose and conditions of use.
- Identify decisions to preserve and justified exceptions; distinguish researched facts from defaults and inferred preferences. Replacement of accepted decisions needs authorization.
- Compare alternatives that differ on meaningful experience criteria using equivalent briefs. Attribute independent contributions and synthesize by criteria rather than vote count; material brief changes require renewed comparison.
- Choose the direction best supporting the primary task.
- Record reusable decisions and open tradeoffs.

## Result

A chosen direction with rationale, constraints, and tradeoffs.

## Scope

Decides direction; does not critique an implementation.

Apply within the authorized local development scope. Choose means from the project's existing conventions. Scale evidence to the consequence of error and disclose material limitations.
