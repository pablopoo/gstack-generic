## Integration Decisions

- Consolidate the new `verify` source into defect correction, with coverage
  criteria in integration handoff. A successful empty run is not verification;
  a post-change failure is not pre-existing merely because its check was untouched.
  Missing environmental prerequisites do not authorize bypasses or unrelated repair.
- Consolidate `runaway-stopper` into scope control, context stewardship and the
  delegation contract. Repeated work needs new evidence, safe owned stopping points,
  and transfer of outstanding obligations. Do not import fixed resource thresholds,
  host lifecycle operations, polling commands or mandatory role rotation.
- Clarify that review reuse depends on the actual coverage selected by current
  inclusion and exclusion policy, not just unchanged candidate content.
- Retain outcome-based method evaluation. Predictions and wording preferences
  cannot establish observed effectiveness; predictions must precede disclosure of
  an independent reference decision. Evaluation does not transfer decision authority.
- Keep required evidence separate from optional review rounds and owner exceptions.
  Existing change-review and handoff guidance already preserve this distinction;
  no mandatory extra reviewers or universal gates are introduced.

Upstream runtime routing, stop hooks, browser/session operations, cloud delivery,
provider selection, model settings and executable verification drivers remain
outside the installed methods. Upstream procedure removals are evidence to assess,
not instructions to delete useful generic investigation criteria automatically.

Independent upstream reconciliation supports these seven method changes without
requiring another method or mandatory gate. The later selection-equivalence fix
supersedes earlier exceptions limited to empty selections: reuse depends on the
actual excluded and retained coverage, not the policy identity alone. New
postmortem and targeted-review examples fit existing causal investigation and
change-review criteria without importing their operational procedures.

Substantive skill, template and supporting-section differences were reviewed;
repeated generated preambles were grouped. Large history and operational
documents received focused section review rather than exhaustive line-by-line
inspection: CHANGELOG, TODOS, the host README, testing internals, coordination,
verification/review/readiness policy, troubleshooting, unattended operation and
host/loadout/budget guides. This is a methodology-scope review, not an audit of
the upstream runtime implementation or a claim that every incidental idea was
exhaustively assessed.

## Validation Limits

Before editing, an independent synthetic exercise applied the existing methods
to four cases: empty verification with a post-edit failure, repetitive workers
near an irreversible operation, wording preference without behavioral trials,
and changed review-selection policy. All four produced appropriate bounded
decisions. The update therefore makes targeted criteria explicit; this exercise
does not establish that the prior version failed or that the new text improves
agent performance. It is not a controlled benchmark or a live workflow test.

A separate post-edit exercise applied the revised methods to the same four
decision classes and returned bounded decisions without treating missing evidence
as success or expanding authority. A read-only review of the seven changed
methods and delegation reference found no concrete contradiction or operational
dependency. Neither exercise establishes improved compliance across other hosts.

Validation: all 48 maintenance tests pass; generated content matches the catalog;
the pinned snapshot check verifies all 68 copied skills and retained legal files;
the diff whitespace check passes. Existing checks cover links, headers, source
dispositions, legal identities and package boundaries. A broader terminology scan
of every installed skill and shared reference found only generic concepts and
explicit limits on installation/publication, not named operational requirements.

The review importer now retains only portable comparison and disposition fields,
rejects common local paths in published text, and omits arbitrary session metadata.
Regression tests cover metadata exclusion, local-path rejection, preservation of
public provenance and decision validation, and the accepted lock's public schema.
These checks do not replace human review for secrets in free text. The 243
decisions and evidence identities are preserved; installed methods are unchanged
by this publication safeguard.

Package publication, the central catalog pin and installed client copies are
separate from this source update. No installation, commit or push is implied.
